      What the critics said

      Consensus summarised from major reviews at launch, scored out of 100.

      Benchmarks

      Single-core and multi-core figures are rounded medians of public Geekbench 6 results; graphics is the Metal score. Real-world speed depends on thermals, memory, and the app — treat these as ballpark, not lab-precise.

      Prices

      US launch price in dollars for the base configuration, before tax, trade-in, or education pricing. Discontinued models are marked; street prices for those are usually well below what is shown.

      Critic scores

      A normalised read of the major English-language reviews published around each device's launch — written summaries, not quotes, and not an official Apple or publisher metric.

      Overall score

      Performance 35% · critical reception 40% · value 25%, where value is multi-core points per dollar. Every part is measured against the best in the same category, so an iPhone is rated against iPhones, not against a Mac Studio. Wearables, audio and home devices are not benchmarked and score on reception 75% and critic-points-per-dollar 25%. Data current to early 2026.
